Woodfine's direct-hold portfolio is intended to expand into Spain as part of the broader [[rollout-index|rollout program]]. This article describes the planned Spain programme; it is not a record of current sites, committed timing, or a finalized regional map.

## Planned scope

The Spain programme is intended to define [[development-regions|development regions]] inside the country — the geographic and jurisdictional zones within which co-location candidates would be evaluated and applicable compliance postures applied. These regions have not yet been formally defined for Spain; this article will be updated once they are.

## Site tracking as the programme matures

Candidate sites identified for Spain will be tracked through the active development pipeline and, where adjacent land is not yet available, the [[about-development-site-monitoring-register|development site monitoring register]], as the [[co-location-methodology|co-location methodology]] identifies qualifying locations. No required development count for a Spain direct-hold vehicle has yet been established; this article does not assert one, and none should be inferred from the counts already documented for other countries.

## Status

The Spain programme's status is planned. This article asserts no site counts, regions, or dates for Spain beyond what is stated here, and will be expanded as the programme's regional structure is formally set.
