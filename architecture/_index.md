Architecture and Design Styles covers the design culture the buildings stand in — the
architectural styles and movements a commercial building can belong to, from classical
through contemporary, and how style, period, and design philosophy bear on a building's
operational suitability and long-term value. [[architecture-overview|Architecture]]
opens the category with the discipline's own long history; from there the coverage runs
from [[classical-architecture|Classical]] and [[gothic-architecture|Gothic]] through
[[bauhaus|Bauhaus]] and [[modern-architecture|Modernism]] to
[[postmodern-architecture|Postmodernism]], [[deconstructivism|Deconstructivism]], and
[[contemporary-architecture|contemporary practice]].

Two articles connect style to place directly: [[new-urbanism|New Urbanism]] describes the
planning framework behind mixed-use, transit-connected neighbourhoods, and
[[contextual-architecture|Contextual Architecture]] covers designing a new building to
its existing surroundings rather than to its own internal logic.
