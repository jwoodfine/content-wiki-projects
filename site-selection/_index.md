How We Choose Sites covers the method behind "near Power Centres" — the discipline that
decides where a building goes before any land changes hands. Articles in this category
cover the retail anchors a site is measured against, the catchment and cluster analysis
that scores a candidate location, and the tests a site must pass before it enters the
development pipeline.

This category also carries the anchor and tenant taxonomy behind the method — Power
Centres, national tenants, and the target hierarchy that ranks a site's retail
neighbours — and the geographic methodology that turns that taxonomy into a score.
