Retail Select places single-storey, street-level retail within the same 1.0-kilometre proximity radius as [[class-professional-centres|Professional Centres]], serving as the retail complement to Woodfine's multi-storey office classes. It is one of the six [[about-development-classes|development classes]] in the direct-hold portfolio Woodfine develops.

## What Retail Select is

Retail Select buildings are single-storey commercial-retail structures, distinct from the multi-storey office format of Professional Centres and Suburban Office. The class accommodates uses such as bank branches and drive-through services — retail and personal-service formats that require ground-level, direct-access premises rather than a multi-floor office fit-out.

## Siting logic: the 1.0-kilometre radius

Retail Select shares the tightest proximity band in the direct-hold portfolio with Professional Centres: positioning within 1.0 kilometre of a [[power-centres|power centre]] anchor. This shared radius is deliberate. Retail Select tenants depend on the pedestrian and vehicle traffic that the power centre anchor and the surrounding professional building population generate, and that dependency is strongest at the closest proximity band the portfolio defines.

## Why the class exists: a campus-complement role

Retail Select is not deployed as a standalone product. Its role is to complement the multi-storey professional building in a campus deployment, occupying street-level positions that a multi-storey office structure does not use efficiently. Where a [[class-professional-centres|Professional Centres]] building anchors the vertical office demand at a site, Retail Select fills the adjacent ground-level positions with retail and personal-service uses that keep the overall site active outside standard office hours and add a further, uncorrelated income stream to the campus.

This complementary role also means Retail Select siting decisions are made in conjunction with the office classes on the same site rather than independently. A site's Retail Select allocation reflects the ground-level retail capacity the campus design supports once the office building footprint is set, not a separate site-selection process of its own.

## Tenant fit

The Retail Select tenant profile centres on uses that require dedicated ground-floor premises and direct vehicle or pedestrian access rather than a suite within a multi-floor building. Target tenants include banks, mobile phone providers, pharmacies, optometrists, personal care operators, jewelry retailers, and health supplement retailers. These tenants typically carry shorter fit-out cycles and different lease structures than the professional office tenants in the same campus, giving Woodfine a further diversification point within a single deployment.

## Food service exclusion

Food service providers are explicitly excluded from Retail Select spaces. This is a deliberate design decision, not an oversight: food service operations generate parking demand, waste management requirements, and ventilation infrastructure that reduce the efficiency of the overall site and impose operational liabilities inconsistent with the Prototype economics that govern all six development classes.
