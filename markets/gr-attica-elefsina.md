Elefsina, an industrial city on the Thriasio Plain west of Athens, holds European Regional
Market standing under the co-location screening framework's composition gate. The framework's
nearest major metropolitan reference point for the market is Athens, 17.9 kilometres away.
Elefsina carries one Regional-tier co-location cluster.

## Overview

According to Wikipedia (accessed 2026-08-25), Elefsina recorded 30,147 municipal residents at
the 2021 census, in West Attica at the northern end of the Saronic Gulf. <!--
wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The city hosts Greece's largest oil refinery
and is recorded in antiquity as the site of the Eleusinian Mysteries and the birthplace of
the playwright Aeschylus; it also carries the older English name Eleusis.

## Regional Market Standing

| Field | Value |

Elefsina has one Regional-tier co-location cluster. As with the neighbouring Gerakas entry,
the catchment population figure reflects the broader Athens metropolitan catchment underlying
the market's strongest member cluster, not a population unique to Elefsina itself. Cluster-level
detail — the specific retail and civic anchors composing the cluster — is pending the
underlying GIS dataset and is not included in this stub. See
