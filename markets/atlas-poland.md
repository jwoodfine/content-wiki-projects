The **Poland regional market index** ranks the 16 Polish entries in the [[atlas-top-400-europe|Top 400 Regional Markets — Europe]] list. Each entry is a named suburb or satellite municipality between 15 and 80 kilometres of a major Polish metropolitan reference point that contains at least one retail co-location cluster, screened and scored under the [[about-regional-markets-system|co-location regional market framework]]. This page is a filtered, Poland-specific view of that continental table — not a separate dataset — so every rank, score, and tier count below matches the continental table exactly and updates automatically if that table is corrected.

## Metropolitan groupings

Poland's 16 markets cluster most heavily around Katowice (4 markets), reflecting the density of the Upper Silesian Industrial Region's suburban belt — the same regional cluster that anchors [[pl-bielsko-biala|Bielsko-Biała]], one of Poland's highest-scoring entries. Kraków (3) and Gdańsk (2) form a second tier. The remaining markets are single entries reporting to Kielce, Bydgoszcz, Łódź, Gliwice, Warsaw, Wrocław, and Łomża.

## Score distribution

Bielsko-Biała (suburb of Katowice, score 12.0) is Poland's highest-scoring entry — two Tier 1 clusters with a civic anchor — followed by Radom (suburb of Kielce, score 10.5, a rare T1+T2+T3 combination with a civic anchor). Toruń and Częstochowa both score 9.0 on a Tier 1 + Tier 2 combination. Most of Poland's remaining entries score 6.0 on a single Tier 1 cluster, and Gdynia and Nowy Sącz score 4.5 on a Tier 2+Tier 3 combination. Poczesna and Rumia are Poland's lowest-ranked entries at 4.0.
