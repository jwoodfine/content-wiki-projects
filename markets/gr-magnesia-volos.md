Volos, a port city on Greece's central-eastern coast, holds European Regional Market
standing under the co-location screening framework's composition gate. The framework's
nearest major metropolitan reference point for the market is Thessaloniki, 141.3 kilometres
away. Volos carries one Regional-tier co-location cluster — the framework's highest
single-cluster tier.

## Overview

According to Wikipedia (accessed 2026-08-25), Volos recorded 85,803 residents in its
municipal unit and 139,670 across the wider municipality at the 2021 census, in the Magnesia
regional unit of Thessaly. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The city
serves as Thessaly's principal sea outlet, with a steel and manufacturing base and the
University of Thessaly anchoring its economy; much of its modern building stock dates from
reconstruction after severe 1955 earthquakes.
