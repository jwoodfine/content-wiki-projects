Chania, the second-largest city on Crete and capital of the Chania regional unit, holds
European Regional Market standing under the co-location screening framework's composition
gate. The framework's nearest major metropolitan reference point for the market is
Heraklion, 103.3 kilometres away. Chania carries one Local-tier co-location cluster.

## Overview

According to Wikipedia (accessed 2026-08-25), Chania recorded 111,375 municipal residents at
the 2021 census, on Crete's northwest coast. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description
--> The city's economy rests on olive and citrus agriculture and on tourism, which expanded
rapidly from the early 1970s onward, layered over a townscape shaped by Minoan, Venetian, and
Ottoman-era influences.
