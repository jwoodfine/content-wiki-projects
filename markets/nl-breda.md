Breda, a North Brabant city positioned equidistant from Rotterdam and Antwerp, ranks 286th
among the 400 European Regional Markets with a composite score of 5.0. A service and
logistics economy anchored by multiple multinational regional headquarters distinguishes it
from a purely residential suburb.

## Overview

According to Wikipedia (accessed 2026-07-10), Breda had a population of 184,126 in 2021,
with a wider metropolitan area of 324,812, across 128.68 square kilometres — the
third-largest municipality in North Brabant after Eindhoven and Tilburg. The city has
transitioned from historical industrial manufacturing to a service-oriented economy built on
business, trade, and logistics, benefiting from its central location between the ports of
Antwerp and Rotterdam. Major international corporations including 3M, Abbott Laboratories,
ExxonMobil, General Electric, and General Motors maintain regional headquarters in Breda,
and the food industry remains significant through Anheuser-Busch InBev and Perfetti Van
Melle. Retail is anchored by the De Barones and 't Sas shopping centres, with markets held
at Grote Markt on Tuesdays and Fridays. The 2020 population was 75.45 percent Dutch, with
immigrant communities from Morocco (3.1 percent), Indonesia (2.9 percent), and Turkey (1.7
percent).
