Alexandroupolis, a port city in northeastern Greece near the Turkish and Bulgarian borders,
holds European Regional Market standing under the co-location screening framework's
composition gate. The framework's nearest major metropolitan reference point for the market
is Plovdiv, Bulgaria, 171.9 kilometres away — a cross-border reference reflecting the city's
position in Thrace, not a commuting relationship. Alexandroupolis combines one District-tier
and one Local-tier co-location cluster.

## Overview

According to Wikipedia (accessed 2026-08-25), Alexandroupolis recorded 71,751 residents at
the 2021 census, and serves as capital of the Evros regional unit in the Eastern Macedonia
and Thrace region. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The city's coastal
position on the Thracian Sea has made it a growing commercial and military port, with
expanded strategic significance for Greece and NATO shipping since 2022.
