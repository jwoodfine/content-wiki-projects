The Markets covers the places, ranked. This category holds the screening universe behind
site selection: the [[atlas-top-400-north-america|Top 400 Regional Markets — North
America]] and the [[atlas-top-400-europe|Top 400 — Europe]], two ranked lists of the
highest-scoring suburban commuter-belt markets on each continent, four hundred each,
scored by [[about-regional-markets-system|a composite of retail co-location tier and
civic-anchor presence]]. Country-level co-location indices and tier indices sit alongside
the two lists and supply the underlying cluster data the ranking is built from.

A small, growing set of individual market profile pages illustrates the shape a full
market article takes — population and economic context, submarkets, and the co-location
clusters driving the score. The two Top 400 lists already cover all four hundred markets
each; individual profile pages exist for a sample of them today, with more commissioned
over time as the programme grows.
