## Screening Universe and Rankings

[[about-regional-markets-system|Regional Markets Intelligence System]] is the reference article for the co-location analysis system, and [[regional-market-definition|Regional Market Definition]] sets out what qualifies a settlement as a Regional Market on the map. [[atlas-top-600-north-america|TOP600 North America]] and [[atlas-top-600-europe|TOP600 Europe]] hold the wider 600-market screening pool each ranked list is drawn from. [[atlas-tier-index-north-america|The Tier Index — North America]] and [[atlas-tier-index-europe|the Tier Index — Europe]] aggregate quality-tier distribution by market. [[atlas-top-400-north-america|The Top 400 — North America]] and [[atlas-top-400-europe|the Top 400 — Europe]] are the two ranked outputs: four hundred highest-scoring suburban commuter-belt markets on each continent, scored by a composite of retail co-location tier and civic-anchor presence.

## Superseded and Retired

Two "Regional Markets" articles predate the current co-location system: [[about-regional-markets|Regional Markets]] and [[about-regional-market-matrix|the Regional Market Matrix]] describe an earlier 8-market direct-hold matrix with a definition of "Regional Market" that directly contradicts the system now in use; both are marked status: superseded. Three country index pages — [[atlas-france|France]], [[atlas-germany|Germany]], and [[atlas-netherlands|Netherlands]] — are marked status: retired, since those countries are deliberately excluded from the current Regional Markets methodology (locked 2026-06-30). All five remain available for historical reference only.
