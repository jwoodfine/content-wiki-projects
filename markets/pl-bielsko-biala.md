Bielsko-Biała, a Silesian Voivodeship city known as "Little Vienna" for its Revivalist and
Art Nouveau architecture, ranks 6th among the 400 European Regional Markets with a composite
score of 12.0. Its two Tier 1 co-location clusters place it among the highest-scoring
markets in the entire European index.

## Overview

According to Wikipedia (accessed 2026-07-10), Bielsko-Biała had approximately 165,127
residents as of December 2024, making it the 22nd-largest city in Poland; the wider
metropolitan area encompasses around 335,000 people across 124.51 square kilometres. The
city sits in southern Poland's Silesian Voivodeship, roughly 48 kilometres from Katowice.
Historically a major textile-industry centre, Bielsko-Biała later developed automotive
manufacturing — the FSM factory produced the Polski Fiat 126p through the 1970s and 1980s —
before the textile sector largely disappeared following the post-1989 economic
transformation. A revitalisation programme begun in 2002 has since brought modern retail
development, including the Galeria Sfera shopping mall built between 2001 and 2009. The city
functions as the administrative, economic, academic, and cultural centre for the surrounding
region and draws tourism through its architectural heritage and proximity to mountain peaks
within city limits.
