Nuremberg does not carry its own entry in the European Top-400 Regional Markets list. In the
current dataset, Nuremberg functions as the metro-core reference point that five other
suburban markets — Bamberg, Erlangen, Kulmbach, Neumarkt in der Oberpfalz, and Forchheim —
are ranked as suburbs *of*, rather than as a suburban market itself.

## Overview

According to Wikipedia (accessed 2026-07-10), Nuremberg's city population was 529,508 in
2024 (urban area 1,374,524; metropolitan region 3,610,543) across 186.46 square kilometres,
the second-largest city in Bavaria, forming a continuous urban region with Fürth, Erlangen,
and Schwabach in the Franconia cultural region. Historically a trade centre, the city became
Bavaria's industrial heart during the 19th century; today Siemens is the largest industrial
employer, alongside MAN. The economy emphasises automation, electrical equipment, medical
technology, and mechanical and optical products, and the city hosts the world's largest toy
fair annually. Roughly a third of Germany's market research agencies operate in Nuremberg.
The medieval city, rebuilt after wartime destruction, draws significant tourism through
heritage attractions and Christmas markets — 3.3 million overnight stays in 2017.

## Regional Market Standing

Nuremberg does not appear as a ranked entry in the [[atlas-top-400-europe|Top 400 Regional
Markets — Europe]] list. This is not a data defect: in the current build, Nuremberg is the
metro-core reference point for five other suburban entries (Bamberg, Erlangen, Kulmbach,
Neumarkt in der Oberpfalz, Forchheim), consistent with
[[about-regional-markets-system|the co-location scoring system's]] own taxonomy excluding
metro cores from the suburban-regional ranked pool.
