Gerakas, an eastern suburb of Athens in the East Attica regional unit, holds European
Regional Market standing under the co-location screening framework's composition gate. The
framework's nearest major metropolitan reference point for the market is Athens, 12.3
kilometres away — the shortest such distance among Greece's seven current entries. Gerakas
carries one Regional-tier co-location cluster.

## Overview

According to Wikipedia (accessed 2026-08-25), Gerakas recorded 33,856 residents at the 2021
census across roughly 6.6 square kilometres, and has formed part of the Pallini municipality
since a 2011 administrative merger. <!-- wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The
area shifted rapidly from farmland to dense suburban housing after infrastructure built for
the 2004 Athens Olympics, and is recorded in antiquity as the birthplace of the philosopher
Epicurus.

## Regional Market Standing

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Metropolitan reference point | Athens |
| Distance | 12.3 km |
| Co-location clusters | 1 Regional-tier |
| Catchment population | 3,547,734 |
| Catchment method | Strongest member cluster (single-cluster fallback) |

Gerakas has one Regional-tier co-location cluster. The catchment population figure reflects
the scale of the broader Athens metropolitan catchment underlying that cluster, not a
population unique to Gerakas itself — a consequence of the framework's disclosed
single-cluster fallback method, not an error. Cluster-level detail — the specific retail and
civic anchors composing the cluster — is pending the underlying GIS dataset and is not
included in this stub. See [[atlas-top-400-europe|Top 400 Regional Markets — Europe]] for the
full Top 400 set and [[co-location-methodology|Co-location Methodology]] for how markets
qualify.
