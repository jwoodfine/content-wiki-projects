The **France regional market index** lists the 102 French entries in the [[atlas-top-400-europe|Top 400 Regional Markets — Europe]] set — the largest single-country share of the European set after Germany. Each entry is a named suburb or satellite municipality between 15 and 80 kilometres of a major French metropolitan reference point that contains at least one retail co-location cluster, qualified under the [[about-regional-markets-system|co-location regional market framework]]. This page is a filtered, France-specific view of that continental table — not a separate dataset — so every entry and tier count below matches the continental table exactly and updates automatically if that table is corrected. No rank or score is published for any market.

## Metropolitan groupings

French markets in this list cluster around a small number of metropolitan reference points rather than spreading evenly across the country. Paris accounts for the largest single grouping (14 markets), reflecting the scale of the Île-de-France commuter belt. Le Havre (9), Lille (8), and Rennes (7) form the next tier, covering Normandy, the Nord-Pas-de-Calais corridor, and Brittany respectively. Lyon (6), Toulouse (5), Nantes (5), and Amiens (5) round out the metropolitan groupings with five or more qualifying suburbs each. The remaining markets are distributed across smaller groupings around Versailles, Valenciennes, Nîmes, Le Mans, Dijon, Strasbourg, Saint-Étienne, Nice, Marseille, Colmar, Reims, Brest, Bordeaux, Grenoble, and Montpellier.

## Tier composition

Auch (suburb of Toulouse) and Beauvais (suburb of Amiens) are France's only two entries combining a Tier 1 and a Tier 2 cluster with a civic anchor. Eighty-six of France's entries qualify on a single Tier 1 cluster with no civic anchor, the most common composition across the entire European set. Seven entries — including [[fr-chambery|Chambéry]] — combine a Tier 1 and a Tier 3 cluster with no civic anchor. Six entries, among them Saint-Nazaire, Valence, and Cholet, combine a Tier 2 and a Tier 3 cluster. La Roche-sur-Yon is France's only entry qualifying on two Tier 3 clusters alone.
