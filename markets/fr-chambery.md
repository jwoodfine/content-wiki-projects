Chambéry, the prefecture of the Savoie department and a major Alpine railway hub, is a
suburb of Grenoble with one Regional-tier and one Local-tier co-location cluster. The city's
character — administrative, educational, and transport-oriented rather than a conventional
retail destination — is atypical among the covered markets that carry a Regional-tier
cluster.

## Overview

According to Wikipedia (accessed 2026-07-10), the Chambéry commune had a population of
59,964 in 2023, with the wider metropolitan area reaching 263,919 in 2022, ranking as
France's 94th-largest city. The commune covers 20.99 square kilometres, with an urban area
of 327.5 square kilometres. Wikipedia's article does not state the distance to Grenoble
directly, but notes Chambéry sits 523 kilometres from Paris and 100 kilometres from Lyon.
The city is a major railway hub at the midpoint of the Franco-Italian Turin–Lyon high-speed
line and hosts university campuses as the seat of the Savoie prefecture. Chambéry holds an
AOC designation for vermouth production, with the Dolin and Routin brands based there — a
regional specialty rather than a national retail draw. Wikipedia's coverage emphasises the
city's historical landmarks and tourism character over modern commercial development.
