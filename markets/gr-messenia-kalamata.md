Kalamata, the capital of Messenia and the second-largest city on the Peloponnese peninsula,
holds European Regional Market standing under the co-location screening framework's
composition gate. The framework's nearest major metropolitan reference point for the market
is Patras, 138.4 kilometres away — well beyond typical commuting range, reflecting Kalamata's
role as its own regional centre rather than a functional suburb. Kalamata carries one
Local-tier co-location cluster.

## Overview

According to Wikipedia (accessed 2026-08-25), Kalamata recorded 72,906 municipal residents
at the 2021 census, on the Nedon River at the head of the Messenian Gulf. <!--
wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The city functions as a working Mediterranean
port with a substantial agricultural export trade, and is internationally known for the
Kalamata olive and olive oil that carry its name.

Kalamata has one Local-tier co-location cluster. **Flagged, not resolved:** the dataset
records Kalamata under the framework's Regional Market composition gate rather than its
separate Standalone Regional Centre category, despite a reference distance (138.4 km) well
outside typical suburban range — an apparent methodology edge case surfaced here rather than
adjudicated. Cluster-level detail — the specific retail and civic anchors composing the
cluster — is pending the underlying GIS dataset and is not included in this stub. See
