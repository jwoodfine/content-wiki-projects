Kanali, a settlement on the island of Corfu in the Ionian Sea, holds European Regional Market
standing under the co-location screening framework's composition gate. The framework's
nearest major metropolitan reference point for the market is Patras, 219.8 kilometres away —
the longest such distance among Greece's seven current entries, and one that crosses open
water to the Greek mainland. Kanali carries one Regional-tier co-location cluster.

## Overview

According to Wikipedia (accessed 2026-08-25), Kanali recorded 4,768 residents at the 2021
census, within the municipality of Central Corfu and the Diapontian Islands. <!--
wikipedia-sourced: population/description --> The settlement functions as a community of the
city of Corfu and has grown steadily from roughly 2,700 residents in 1991, reflecting gradual
island development rather than rapid metropolitan expansion.

## Regional Market Standing

Kanali has one Regional-tier co-location cluster. **Flagged, not resolved:** Corfu has no land
connection to Patras, so the 219.8 km reference distance measures the framework's nearest
continental reference point, not a commuting distance — a reminder that this field is a
screening-algorithm reference, not an administrative or commuter relationship. Cluster-level
detail — the specific retail and civic anchors composing the cluster — is pending the
underlying GIS dataset and is not included in this stub. See
