Why Places Grow covers the city-scale forces behind demand. [[commuter|Commuter (PKS)]]
and [[urban-fringe|Urban Fringe (VWH)]] are two of the three Location Intelligence
co-location archetypes — transit-adjacent sites with significant parking, and
large-format retail and distribution-industrial activity on the metropolitan periphery,
respectively. [[optimum-mosaic-demographic-profiling|Optimum Mosaic demographic
profiling]] and [[regional-market-demographic-threshold|the Regional Market demographic
threshold]] cover the population signals that separate a qualifying market from a
local-serving community, and [[parking-ratio-standards|parking ratio standards]] set the
stall-per-square-foot requirement every site must meet before construction.
