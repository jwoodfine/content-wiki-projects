How We Build covers the design system behind every Woodfine building: designing from the
tenant out, the fixed floor plate, the offset core, and the key-plan-and-tile geometry
that turns occupier requirements into repeatable construction. Articles in this category
also cover measurement standards — including the BOMA standard — building
certifications, and the BIM practice that documents the design system digitally.

The category also covers the disciplines that turn that leasing geometry into a
buildable, sustainable site: the [[design-sequence-priority|design sequence priority]]
that orders Leasing Plan, Building Services, and Structural design; the
[[four-to-one-parking-ratio|4:1 parking ratio]] and
[[water-management-system|water management system]] applied at the site level; and the
[[common-building-code|Common Building Code]],
[[tile-level-climate-zoning|tile-level climate zoning]], and
[[structural-bay-depth-discipline|structural bay depth discipline]] applied at the
building and leasehold level.
