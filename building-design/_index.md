How We Build covers the design system behind every Woodfine building: designing from the
tenant out, the fixed floor plate, the offset core, and the key-plan-and-tile geometry
that turns occupier requirements into repeatable construction. Articles in this category
also cover measurement standards — including the BOMA standard — building
certifications, and the BIM practice that documents the design system digitally.
